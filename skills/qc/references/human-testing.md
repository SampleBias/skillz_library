# Human testing and completion

For `READY_FOR_HUMAN_TESTING`, require integrated project or milestone acceptance, applicable mandatory automated and independent verification, documented residual risks, and an actionable, reproducible procedure. Identify the exact candidate, environment, prerequisites, setup, test steps, expected results and how to report discrepancies. Note unsafe or irreversible actions and any required authorization. If evidence or procedure is missing, remain blocked and explain what is needed.

When the procedure has been handed to the human and their results are outstanding, record `AWAITING_HUMAN_TEST`. Never perform or invent the human's acceptance. Record supplied results, candidate identity, unresolved discrepancies and test date. A failure returns affected work to repair and fresh verification; a change after testing invalidates affected test evidence.

Use `COMPLETE` only when agreed project requirements are met, required tests and QC pass for the final candidate, outstanding issues are resolved or explicitly accepted by the authorized human, and that human explicitly accepts the testing results and signs off completion. Preserve the sign-off and its scope in the report. Readiness, a successful automated run, or a checkpoint push alone does not imply completion.
